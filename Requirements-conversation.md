## 1. Stakeholders
- Who uses the system directly?
- Who maintains it?
- Who approves it?
- Who is affected indirectly?
- Are there hidden stakeholders (legal, IT, support)?

## 2. Requirement Classification
### Functional (F)
What must the system DO?

Examples:
- create
- update
- delete
- book
- cancel
- notify

### Non-Functional (NFR)
How WELL must it work?

Check for:
- performance
- availability
- usability
- security
- reliability
- multilingual support

Flag vague words:
- fast
- easy
- user-friendly
- secure

Always ask for measurable values.

### Constraints (C)
What limits the solution?

Check:
- deadline
- budget
- technology restrictions
- legal requirements
- hosting limitations

## 3. Ambiguity Check
Look for unclear words:
- fast
- simple
- soon
- often
- secure
- easy

Ask:
“How do we measure this?”

## 4. Hidden Assumptions
Ask:
- What is being assumed but not stated?
- Are workflows already known?
- Are user roles clearly defined?

## 5. Contradictions
Check for conflicting requirements.

Examples:
- offline vs online login
- security vs ease of use
- zero budget vs hard deadline

## 6. Risks
- missing stakeholder
- unclear deadline
- no acceptance criteria
- legal risks
- technical dependencies

## 7. Follow-up Questions
Always prepare questions for:
- vague terms
- missing numbers
- role permissions
- failure cases
- edge cases
